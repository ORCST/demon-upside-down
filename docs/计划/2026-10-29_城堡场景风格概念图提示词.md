# 城堡内部场景：风格概念图提示词（给 GPT 生图）

目的：用户觉得 v01 场景（KayKit 3D 模块渲染）画风不对。先用 GPT 生成几张“游戏截图样张”定风格，选定后再决定场景怎么做（继续 3D 渲染调风格，或改成手绘图块）。

## 用法

1. 附上参考图：
   - 牛来：`art_source/reference/poses/niulai_hd_idle.png`（必须附，保证角色比例和画风对齐）
   - 当前版本截图（可选，让 GPT 知道布局）：`outputs/game_test_devin2/castle_frames2.png`
2. 复制下面的主提示词。想要不同方向时，把最后的“风格方向”那段换成 A / B / C 中的一个，每个方向各生成一张。

## 主提示词（复制这段）

```
Create a mock-up in-game screenshot for a 2D side-scrolling pixel art Metroidvania (Castlevania-like), set INSIDE a dark castle.

Technical format:
- Exactly 16:9, designed at a native resolution of 640x360 pixels and scaled up 2x with nearest-neighbor (1280x720 output). One consistent square pixel grid, crisp pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients smoothing, no photographic lighting.
- Strict side view (orthographic, like a classic 2D platformer). No perspective vanishing points, no isometric angle.

Main character (use the attached image as the exact design): Niu Lai, a small chubby yellow calf with a big round hornless head, cream muzzle, one thick dark eyebrow, pink inner ear, brown shorts and belt, short tail, holding a large plain steel-gray greatsword. He stands on the floor in the left third of the frame, facing right, about 64 native pixels tall (about 1/5.5 of the screen height). Keep his colors and proportions exactly; he must be the most readable thing on screen.

Scene composition (layers, back to front):
1. Far background: tall castle hall wall with gothic arched windows showing a deep night sky and a pale moon, hanging red banners, chains, wall torches with warm flickering light.
2. Mid background: a row of stone columns / arches, slightly darker, giving depth.
3. Gameplay layer: a solid stone floor across the bottom ~60 native px of the screen, one raised stone step, and two thin one-way wooden platforms floating at different heights. The walkable surfaces must clearly read as solid and different from the background (stronger contrast, clear top edge).
4. A few foreground silhouette elements at the very edges (a dark pillar or hanging chain) to add depth, not covering the character.
- Add one simple enemy on the right side for scale (a small goblin-like demon, about the same height as Niu Lai).
- A minimal HUD: a row of small red health pips at the top left.

Color and light: limited palette (about 24-32 colors). Background desaturated and darker, cool tones; torchlight warm orange; gameplay surfaces medium contrast; the yellow calf pops against the background. Light comes from the upper left.

Mood: a birthday-gift game, slightly goofy but adventurous. Dark castle, not horror.

Style direction: <STYLE>
```

## 风格方向（替换 `<STYLE>`）

- **A. 经典恶魔城（推荐先试）**
  `Classic 16-bit era Castlevania: Symphony of the Night inspired. Rich hand-drawn stone textures with clear brick shading, gothic ornament, deep purples and blues, painterly but strictly pixel art.`
- **B. 现代精致像素**
  `Modern high-detail pixel art like Dead Cells / Blasphemous backgrounds: atmospheric depth haze between layers, rim light, dramatic warm-vs-cool contrast, but still crisp pixels.`
- **C. 可爱卡通**
  `Cute, rounded, chunky cartoon pixel art matching the calf's soft style: thick dark outlines on gameplay surfaces, simple shapes, warm friendly colors with a purple night tint, like a cozy indie platformer.`

## 看图时重点比较

- 牛来站在场景里是否协调（比例、轮廓线、亮度）。
- 地面和平台能不能一眼看出是“能踩的”。
- 背景是否抢戏。
- 细节密度：每块砖画多细、有没有描边。
