## 1. 主角像素规范（所有主角像素图通用）

生成每张主角姿势图时，都把下面这段英文和该姿势的要求一起使用：

```text
GLOBAL RULES (apply to every Niu Lai sprite):
- The attached reference image is the FINAL approved sprite of "Niu Lai" (yellow calf greatsword warrior) in his idle stance, on the standard canvas. Keep his identity exactly: hornless lumpy head, golden yellow skin, pale cream muzzle and chin, small pointed ear with pink inside, ONE thick dark furrowed eyebrow, sleepy deadpan eye, soft chubby childlike body with round belly and short limbs, bare yellow stubby hands, dark brown hooves, dark brown cloth shorts, brown leather belt, and a short thin yellow cow tail with a dark brown tuft at the tip, coming out from the back of the shorts. The tail is always present; it may swing naturally with the pose.
- The greatsword is the same as in the reference: wide plain steel-gray blade with ONE lighter edge line, dark crossguard, brown grip. Same size; never shorten, lengthen or redesign it.
- True pixel art on a NATIVE 256x256 canvas, exported EXACTLY 4x nearest-neighbor to 1024x1024. One consistent square pixel grid, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no half pixels, no partial transparency.
- Use ONLY this 17-color palette, no new colors:
  #281B21 #3F2B25 #4D515D #5B402D #777C86 #7E5B3B #A78150 #B68A24 #B6A385 #BCC3CA #DCCCAB #DD9586 #E8B830 #F2BAA3 #F4D36A #F6EACB #F8F4EA
- One-native-pixel dark outline (#281B21) around the whole character and the sword. Light from the top-left, 2-3 flat tones per material.
- STRICT right-facing true profile: body and head point RIGHT, only one eye visible, muzzle points right. (Unless the pose says otherwise.)
- Same scale as the reference: the character is about 64 native pixels tall when standing upright. Match the BODY to the reference (same head size, same muzzle size, same limb thickness). Do NOT rescale the whole image to make the sword length match; body size consistency is more important than exact blade length.
- GROUND POSES: the soles of the hooves stand on native row y=207 (ground line y=208), exactly like the reference. The character's body stays centered around native x=112.
- AIR POSES: keep the body centered around native x=112, with the lowest hoof around native y=170.
- Everything (character and the whole sword) stays inside the canvas with at least 8 native pixels of margin.
- NO motion blur, NO slash trails, NO speed lines, NO impact effects, NO ground, NO shadow, NO text. Fully transparent background.
```

## 2. 主角 · 牛来

### T0 ★✅ 补尾巴（最先做，做完就是新基准图）

文件名：`niulai_idle_tail.png`（1024×1024）+ `niulai_idle_tail_native.png`（256×256）

原因：动画测试里，MiniMax H3 每次都会自己给牛来"长出"一条尾巴。干脆让尾巴成为设定的一部分，以后每一帧就都一致了。

参考图：旧基准图 `niulai_idle_canvas256_x4.png`

```text
Edit the attached pixel-art sprite of "Niu Lai" (yellow calf greatsword warrior). ADD ONE THING ONLY: a short, thin cow tail.

Tail:
- Comes out from the back of his dark brown shorts, just below the belt, on the LEFT side of the sprite (his back side, since he faces right)
- Thin yellow tail (same golden yellow skin colors as his body), about 14-18 native pixels long, hanging down and curving slightly backward and outward in a relaxed S-shape
- Ends in a small dark brown tuft of hair (3-4 native pixels), like a real calf tail
- Same 1-native-pixel dark outline as the rest of the sprite
- It must not touch or overlap the sword

Everything else must stay PIXEL-FOR-PIXEL IDENTICAL to the attached image: same pose, same position on the canvas, same face, same outfit, same sword, same outline, same palette. Only the tail pixels are new.

True pixel art on a NATIVE 256x256 canvas, exported EXACTLY 4x nearest-neighbor to 1024x1024. Use ONLY these 17 colors:
#281B21 #3F2B25 #4D515D #5B402D #777C86 #7E5B3B #A78150 #B68A24 #B6A385 #BCC3CA #DCCCAB #DD9586 #E8B830 #F2BAA3 #F4D36A #F6EACB #F8F4EA
No anti-aliasing, no partial transparency. Fully transparent background, no ground, no shadow, no text.
Save both the 1024x1024 image and the 256x256 native image.
```

POSE: mid-stride running pose (ground pose). Front leg reaching forward, back leg pushing off behind, body leaning forward. He carries the greatsword with both hands low at his side, blade trailing BEHIND him, pointing back and slightly down, tip well above the ground. Determined deadpan face.

**P03 起跳** `niulai_jump`

**P04 下落** `niulai_fall`
```text
POSE: falling down (air pose). Legs extended downward ready to land, arms raised a little, greatsword held with both hands in front, blade pointing forward and up like the idle stance. Ear blown upward.
```

**P05 落地** `niulai_land`
```text
POSE: landing impact (ground pose). Deep crouch, knees strongly bent, body compressed, greatsword held forward and slightly down with both hands, blade nearly horizontal.
```

**P06 冲刺** `niulai_dash`
```text
POSE: fast forward dash (ground pose). Very low forward-leaning lunge, body almost horizontal, front leg far forward, back leg stretched behind. Greatsword held low with both hands, blade pointing straight back behind him, parallel to the ground.
```

**P07 横斩收势** `niulai_slash1_end`
```text
POSE: END of a horizontal forward slash (ground pose). The blade points straight FORWARD, horizontal, at chest height, arms fully extended forward, front knee bent, back leg straight. Body leaning forward into the swing.
```

**P08 上撩收势** `niulai_slash2_end`
```text
POSE: END of a rising upward slash (ground pose). He has swung the greatsword up from low in front to high above: both hands now above his head, blade pointing UP and slightly BACK over his head. Body stretched upward, weight on the front foot.
```

**P09 下劈终结收势** `niulai_slash3_end` ★
```text
POSE: END of a heavy overhead smash (ground pose). He has swung the greatsword from over his head down in front of him: the blade points forward and DOWN, the tip touching the ground line in front of his front hoof. Both hands still on the grip, arms extended forward and down. Body leaning far forward, front knee deeply bent, back leg stretched behind. The back hoof stays planted in the same place as in the reference.
```

**P10 蓄力举剑** `niulai_heavy_windup`
```text
POSE: charging a heavy attack (ground pose). The greatsword is raised high over and BEHIND his head with both hands, blade pointing back and down behind his back. Body leaning back, knees bent, braced, gritting teeth.
```

**P11 上挑收势** `niulai_upslash_end`
```text
POSE: END of a powerful upward launching slash (ground pose). The blade points STRAIGHT UP above him, arms fully raised overhead, body fully stretched upward, standing on the tip of the front hoof, back hoof lifting off the ground.
```

**P12 空中斩收势** `niulai_air_slash_end`
```text
POSE: END of a midair slash (air pose). Knees tucked, body curled forward, the blade swung forward and down, pointing about 45 degrees below horizontal in front of him.
```

**P13 空中下插** `niulai_plunge`
```text
POSE: plunging downward attack (air pose, body a bit higher so the sword fits). He holds the greatsword with both hands close to his chest, blade pointing STRAIGHT DOWN below his body, knees pulled up, looking down. The sword tip stays inside the canvas.
```

**P14 受击** `niulai_hurt`
```text
POSE: getting hit (ground pose). Recoiling BACKWARD (to the left), head thrown back, eye squeezed shut, mouth open in pain, front hoof lifted off the ground, greatsword held loosely low in front with both hands.
```

**P15 倒地** `niulai_down`
```text
POSE: knocked out, lying flat on his back on the ground line, head on the right side, eye closed (a little swirl-dizzy look is fine), arms spread. The greatsword lies on the ground beside him. Everything stays on or above the ground line y=208.
```
